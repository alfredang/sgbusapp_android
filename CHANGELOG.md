# Changelog

## 1.1 (versionCode 2) — 2026-11-04

- Renamed the app to **Bus Arrival Times**.
- Added data-source credit ("Bus arrival data: LTA DataMall", linking to datamall.lta.gov.sg) under the arrivals list and in a new About screen.
- Added an independence notice: not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by LTA, SBS Transit, SMRT, Tower Transit or Go-Ahead.
- Clearer message when no buses are expected at a stop (e.g. overnight).
- Release builds are now minified with R8.

## 1.0 (versionCode 1)

- Live bus arrivals for popular Singapore bus stops.
