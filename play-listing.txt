APP NAME (17/30)
Bus Arrival Times

SHORT DESCRIPTION (≤80)
Live arrival times for Singapore bus stops, with seat availability.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Bus Arrival Times shows when the next buses will reach your stop, so you can head out at the right time.

Features
• Live arrival estimates for the next three buses on every service at a stop
• Seat availability for each bus: seats available, standing, or limited standing
• Bus type: single-deck, double-deck or bendy
• Search stops by stop code, road name or landmark
• One-tap refresh
• No account, no sign-in, no ads

Data source
Bus arrival data comes from LTA DataMall (https://datamall.lta.gov.sg), the Land Transport Authority's open data service. Arrival times are estimates and may differ from actual bus arrivals.

Independent app
Bus Arrival Times is an independent app developed by Tertiary Infotech Academy Pte Ltd. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Land Transport Authority (LTA), SBS Transit, SMRT, Tower Transit or Go-Ahead.
